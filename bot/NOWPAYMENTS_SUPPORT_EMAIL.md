## Where to Send:

- Email: support@nowpayments.io
- Or use the contact form on: https://nowpayments.io/help
- Or create a support ticket in the dashboard (if you can access it)

---

## Tips:

1. **Check your email first**: Search your inbox for emails from "nowpayments.io" - you might find registration confirmation or previous communications
2. **Try password reset**: Go to https://nowpayments.io/login and click "Forgot Password"
3. **Check different emails**: Try all email addresses you might have used
4. **Check browser saved passwords**: Your browser might have saved the password
